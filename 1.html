From the city centre, the direct bus takes about two hours by way of Glenrothes. The airport link is weaker still: the direct bus runs about every two hours by way of Kinross, and the official advice for Open week from the airport needs a tram, a train and a shuttle.

Ideas from places I have travelled

Spain

Go straight from the airport

ALSA runs direct coaches from Madrid Barajas Airport Terminal 4 to cities such as Salamanca, a university city, without going into Madrid first.

For St Andrews: serve Edinburgh Airport directly on every journey, so arriving students and golfers never have to travel into the city to go north.

Dubrovnik, Croatia

Time the coach to the flights

The Dubrovnik airport shuttle leaves about 30 minutes after every flight arrival, and its timetable changes between the quieter months and peak summer.

For St Andrews: time airport departures after the busiest arrival waves, and publish separate timetables for term time, holidays and the golf season.

How I would test it before launch

  1. Ride the corridor. Travel the JET 787, the X59 and the train to Leuchars in the first week of term and count passengers and luggage at each stop.
  2. Match the airport. Pull Edinburgh Airport's arrival times for September, January and July, and set departures around the busiest waves.
  3. Talk to the people who know. Speak to the university and the Students' Association about arrival days, and meet Fife Council officers before formal consultation.
  4. Check the fleet and charging. Confirm how many coaches and drivers the Kirkcaldy hub can spare at each peak, and where coaches charge between shifts.
  5. Build and register. Model the timetable by season in a spreadsheet, test it against historical demand, then register it with the Traffic Commissioner.
