Project 02 · Root cause analysis

Why the airport bus only runs every 2 hours

St Andrews has over 10,000 students, hosts The Open and has no railway station. From Edinburgh Airport, the direct bus runs about every 2 hours. I used the official timetables and the rules on drivers' hours to find out why, then proposed St Andrews Express: an electric coach timed to flights, term dates and the golf season.

01 · The problem

Almost 2 hours at the bus stop

A student lands at Edinburgh Airport on move in day and reaches the bus stop at 11:00. The 10:45 JET 787 has just left.

12:45Next direct bus

It reaches St Andrews at 14:12: almost 2 hours of waiting with suitcases after a long flight.5

05 · Root cause

Not traffic. Coaches and drivers.

I tested each likely cause of the 2 hour gap against the published timetable and the official rules on drivers' hours.

Rush hour traffic

The gap is the same at 03:00, on empty roads, as at 17:00. Traffic adds 15 minutes to the 16:45 journey, but does not change how often the bus runs.5

Not the cause

Driver breaks

On routes over 50 km, a driver can drive for 4.5 hours before a 45 minute break, which can be split into 15 and 30 minutes, and for up to 9 hours a day.11,12 The coach waits 33 to 53 minutes at St Andrews before every return journey, which leaves room for these breaks.

Built into the timetable

Coaches and drivers on the route

Every weekday journey fits a pattern of two coaches shuttling back and forth. Each round trip takes about 4 hours including waiting time, so two coaches give one bus every 2 hours.

Main cause

Why only two coaches

A timetable cannot show whether there are too few passengers to fill more coaches, or too few drivers to run them.

To test first

Traffic adds time, not buses

Both direct buses are given more time for the same journey at the evening peak. How often they run stays the same.

Scheduled weekday running times to St Andrews Bus Station.5,6 Towards Edinburgh the morning peak does the same: the 07:00 JET 787 from St Andrews is given 1 hr 45, against 1 hr 35 at midday.

Two coaches run every weekday journey

My reading of the weekday JET 787 timetable.5 Each airport departure leaves 9 to 20 minutes after a coach arrives from St Andrews.

Stagecoach does not publish which coach or driver works each journey, and some coaches may also work other routes.

07 · The proposal

St Andrews Express: a direct bus about every hour

An electric coach linking St Andrews, Cupar, Edinburgh Airport and Edinburgh, leaving the airport between JET 787 departures.

✕Today
  1. A direct airport bus about every 2 hours in the daytime
  2. About an hour's average wait at the airport
  3. The city centre bus takes about 2 hours via Glenrothes
  4. Open week advice from the airport needs a tram, a train and a shuttle

2 coaches on the JET 787

✓With St Andrews Express
  1. A direct bus from the airport about every hour, timed between JET 787 departures
  2. Average wait falls to around 30 minutes
  3. Extra journeys timed to flight arrivals at the busiest points of the year
  4. Airport and city centre on one route, with the city centre as the last stop

3 new coaches to start

Recommended rollout

01

A coach every 2 hours, plus peaks

Run 3 coaches, leaving the airport between JET 787 departures, so airport passengers get a direct bus about every hour. Add journeys timed to flight arrivals in September, at the semester changes, at graduation and for the Open.

02

Count before adding more

Count passengers at those peaks to see whether coaches fill and people are left behind.

03

Go hourly when drivers are there

An hourly service needs about twice the coaches and driving hours, so it depends on how many drivers the Kirkcaldy hub recruits.8

09 · Benchmarking

Ideas from places I've travelled

Spain

Make the airport a main stop

ALSA runs direct coaches from Madrid Barajas Airport Terminal 4 to cities such as Salamanca, a university city, so airport passengers do not need to travel into Madrid first.9

For St Andrews

The JET 787 already links the airport to St Andrews, but only every 2 hours. Serving the airport and the city centre on one route lets each coach carry both flight passengers and city travellers, which helps fill the extra journeys. The city centre is the last stop, after the airport, so flight passengers are not slowed down.

Dubrovnik, Croatia

Time the coach to the flights

The Dubrovnik airport shuttle leaves about 30 minutes after every flight arrival, and its timetable changes between the quieter months and peak summer.10

For St Andrews

Time airport departures after the busiest arrival waves, and publish separate timetables for term time, holidays and the golf season.

10 · Before launch

Recommended validation

Five checks to run before any new service is proposed.

  1. Ride the corridor

    Travel the JET 787, the X59 and the train to Leuchars in the first week of term. Count passengers and luggage at each stop, and note any journey that leaves people behind.

  2. Match the airport

    Pull Edinburgh Airport's arrival times for September, January and July, and set departures around the busiest waves.

  3. Talk to the people who know

    Speak to the university and the Students' Association about arrival days, and meet Fife Council officers before formal consultation.

  4. Check the fleet and charging

    Test the coach estimate against Ember's own running times, confirm how many coaches and drivers the Kirkcaldy hub can spare at each peak, and where coaches charge between shifts.

  5. Build and register

    Model the timetable by season in a spreadsheet, test it against historical demand, then register it with the Traffic Commissioner.
