St Andrews has over 10,000 students, hosts The Open and has no railway station. From Edinburgh Airport the direct bus runs about every 2 hours. I found out why, then proposed a fix.

A bus every 2 hours means about an hour’s wait. Hourly would cut it to about 30 minutes.

02

Not traffic. Coaches and drivers.

  1. 01Rush hour traffic: not the cause. The gap is the same at 03:00 as at 17:00. Traffic adds time, not buses.
  2. 02Driver breaks: built in. The coach waits 33 to 53 minutes at St Andrews, room for the legal breaks.
  3. 03Coaches and drivers: the main cause. Every weekday journey fits 2 coaches shuttling back and forth.
  4. 04Why only 2: too few passengers or too few drivers? The timetable can’t show it, so test it first.

03

St Andrews Express: a direct bus about every hour

Today

  • A direct airport bus about every 2 hours
  • About an hour’s average wait
  • Open week advice: tram, train and shuttle

With St Andrews Express

  • A direct bus about every hour, timed between JET 787 departures
  • Average wait around 30 minutes
  • Extra journeys at the busiest points of the year
